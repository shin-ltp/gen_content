Grupo Bimbo collaborated with J.P. Morgan Payments to strengthen its payments and treasury operations, with the goal of improving visibility, efficiency and control in a high-scale, complex operating environment.

For more than 80 years, Grupo Bimbo has come from a bakery in Mexico City to a global leading baking company and a key player in snacks. Throughout this story, its mission has been clear, to deliver delicious and nutritious food for everyone. There is a philosophy that guides every decision, that's why Grupo Bimbo aims to be a sustainable, as well as a highly productive and fully human company.

Making our products is a shared effort that covers the entire value chain in order to reach the hands of our consumers, from field producers to our customers and commercial partners. Our global presence requires integrated, secure and agile financial processes. That's why Grupo Bimbo has strategic partners, such as J.P. Morgan, in order to keep on boosting a sustainable growth.

Our global expertise tailored to Mexico, allows us to work with Grupo Bimbo in order to manage the payments through different banks and currencies. We provide them real-time visibility of their cash flow and digital tools that simplifies payments on time and creates more informed decisions.

Our joined efforts with Grupo Bimbo allows us to predict changes, to explore new markets and to remain competitive, while supporting their growth with adaptable and resilient payment solutions.

This strategic approach is reflected in every decision that we make. Our operations are becoming smoother and safer, which allows us to strengthen our execution and respond more precisely to the needs of the business.

J.P. Morgan Payments as a strategic partner is key to continue strengthening our capabilities.

Supporting Grupo Bimbo in its growth is also to support its goals. Every decision that we make together reflects the profound commitment to build a wealthier future, to improve people’s quality of life, and to leave a meaningful impression for generations to come.

With distributed operations, as well as multiple collections and multicurrency payment flows, Grupo Bimbo sought to evolve its treasury model to respond more deftly to business needs. Working alongside J.P. Morgan Payments, the company advanced its journey toward a more standardized payments architecture with greater end-to-end traceability. These efforts helped to strengthen operational control, improve visibility and support more informed decision-making. The result was a stronger foundation to operate consistently at scale and enable continuous improvements in efficiency and operational resilience.

As an organization with broad and demanding operations, Grupo Bimbo faced the challenge of managing payment flows with multiple stakeholders, calendars and operational needs, while maintaining high standards of control and continuity. The team identified opportunities to reduce operational friction, increase visibility across the payments cycle and strengthen process consistency across regions and business units without losing the flexibility to address local requirements.

In that context, the priority was to evolve toward a model that would help enable end-to-end traceability, reconciliation and operational risk management, with a more consistent experience for internal teams and a stronger foundation for growth.

To address these challenges, Grupo Bimbo collaborated with J.P. Morgan Payments on an approach focused on standardization, connectivity and operational control.

The joint work included coordination among teams—including treasury, finance, IT, operations and internal controls—to ensure a rollout aligned to governance, security and continuity requirements. In particular, mechanisms were defined to support traceability and monitoring, along with an implementation approach that balanced operational impact and speed of adoption.

Ongoing efforts to optimize treasury and payments processes have helped Grupo Bimbo enhance visibility, standardization, operational efficiency, control and resilience. By increasing transparency into payment status, reconciliation and operational performance, the company continues to support informed decision-making, strengthen operational oversight and drive greater efficiency across its global operations.

Beyond day-to-day performance, the initiative also established a foundation to continue evolving treasury capabilities. enabling additional enhancements as business priorities and the operating environment change.
