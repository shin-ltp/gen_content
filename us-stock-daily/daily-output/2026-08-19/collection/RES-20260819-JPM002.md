FinClear is one of Australia’s leading full-service providers of listed securities, offering clearing, execution and related services to more than 250 stockbrokers, FinTechs, financial advisors and wealth managers. As a trusted counterparty to more than 2 million Australian retail investor accounts, FinClear administers more than AUD 200 billion of assets on platform.1

With a market-leading position in the clearing and execution of Australian equities, FinClear is focused on delivering infrastructure that helps clients meet changing investor needs across domestic and international markets.

As Australian investors continued to diversify into global assets, FinClear saw rising demand from clients for faster, more cost-effective international trading and settlement capabilities.

The shift to T+1 settlement for U.S. equities made this need more urgent. Wholesale brokers required same-day FX settlement capabilities, with funds needing to reach U.S. brokers within approximately 24 hours of trade execution. Australia’s 14-hour time difference with the U.S. further tightened the settlement window.

FinClear needed a scalable FX and payments solution tailored to the wealth management sector—one that could simplify international trading, reduce manual work and help clients deliver a better investor experience.

“It’s about putting cash, FX and payments at the center of the investing workflow. We needed a flexible, scalable solution. That’s why we chose J.P. Morgan.”

J.P. Morgan Payments helped FinClear develop FinClear Cash Hub, a fully integrated FX and payments solution for Australia’s wealth sector. Built on FinClear’s proprietary technology, the platform is supported by the global banking infrastructure, offshore banking capabilities and API connectivity of J.P. Morgan. Together, those capabilities give FinClear a more automated way to manage international settlement at scale.

FinClear Cash Hub brings several core capabilities into one operating model.

Each end investor receives a unique multicurrency account  number, helping improve reconciliation efficiency.

Tiered FX and interest pricing allow FinClear to tailor spreads and interest tiers based on trade size and currency, while client segmentation tools give operations staff and advisors secure, user-specific oversight of client transactions.

Direct API integration with payment capabilities from J.P. Morgan also reduces manual reconciliation and re-keying across FinClear’s core platform.

The solution extends beyond platform connectivity. Offshore banking capabilities from J.P. Morgan helped FinClear establish accounts in key markets, including the U.S. and Canada, enabling local payments and bulk sweeping of foreign currency balances. That reduces the need for costly individual SWIFT transfers and supports faster movement of funds across markets.
