## 内容

If going to the movies seems to cost a fortune these days, you&#x27;re not imagining it. The cost of entertainment has been increasing more than many other segments of the market.

The cost of admission to sporting events (up 6.2% year-over-year), movies, theaters and concerts (up 3.6%) and buying or renting video games (up 14.2%) costs more than it did a year ago, according to the consumer price index for June.

These price hikes aren&#x27;t a recent phenomenon. The average ticket price was $132.62 in 2025, up 37% from $96.17 in 2019, according to Pollstar . A separate study comparing prices at the 20 most popular theme parks in North America found the average price of a single-day ticket rose to $105 in 2025, up 37% from $76 in 2015.

Regardless of what you have planned for the summer, there&#x27;s a chance your credit card can help you save — whether you&#x27;re applying for a new card or taking advantage of hidden benefits from cards you already own.

It&#x27;s possible that the same card you use for groceries could also be earning you cash back on some of your fun purchases as well. Even if you don&#x27;t currently have any of these cards, they&#x27;re all no-annual-fee options if you&#x27;re interested in applying.

Capital One considers entertainment purchases to include movie theaters, amusement parks, professional and semi-professional live sports events and more. No matter what your favorite pastime is, you&#x27;ll probably earn rewards.

Many major card issuers operate their own experience programs or portals, which can provide access to unique events and invites to exclusive pop-ups. Depending on the type of card you have, you can also redeem rewards toward these experiences.

With Chase Experiences , you can redeem points for Fandango gift cards if there&#x27;s a movie you&#x27;ve been meaning to see, or a Topgolf gift card if you&#x27;re looking for a fun activity. Gift cards aren&#x27;t always the most valuable redemption option — through Chase, redeeming for gift cards is often worth 1 cent per point — but they can be a simple way to help offset costs.

While these programs can help you get into certain events, it&#x27;s likely going to be at the current market value, not any cheaper. There are some exceptions, like the MLB&#x27;s partnership with Capital One . Each MLB game has four cardholder-exclusive seats, and they only cost 5,000 miles or $40 in Capital One cash back, which is usually a significant discount.

Tip: Some issuers offer a separate portal for booking more premium experiences, such as Chase&#x27;s Sapphire Reserve Experiences and American Express&#x27; Amex Experiences. From getting exclusive behind-the-scenes access to popular TV shows to an ultimate Napa Valley weekend retreat, these experiences are designed for unique access rather than saving money.
